How to play

A classic match lasts 10 minutes. Most players start as survivors and a few start as zombies (about 1 zombie for every 5 players). Every survivor who gets caught becomes a zombie, so the horde keeps growing.

  1. Find out your role

    Each player learns if they are a survivor, a doctor or a zombie. The first zombie is Patient Zero and gets a short story about how it all started.

  2. Survivors run

    "Run! Zombies are released in…" Survivors get a few seconds (5 by default) to spread out before the zombies are let loose.

  3. Zombies hunt

    A zombie that touches a survivor bites them. The survivor is frozen for 10 seconds. If a doctor reaches them and touches them in time, they are cured. If not, they turn into a zombie.

  4. Survivors work

    Every map has task stations. Each task a survivor finishes takes 15 seconds off everyone's timer and earns them 15 coins.

  5. Who wins?

    Survivors win if at least one of them is still human when the timer hits zero. Zombies win if they infect everyone before that.

Four roles

Every match has two teams. The survivor team also has doctors (2 by default), and the zombie team starts with Patient Zero.

Survivor

Last until the end

  • Run from zombies and hide behind walls and crates.
  • Do tasks to make the timer shorter.
  • You have one stone to throw. Zombies hear a noise where it lands and go there.
  • Zombies see blue sound waves from survivors near them, so sometimes it is smarter to stand still.
Doctor

Save bitten players

  • A doctor is a survivor with the power to heal.
  • When someone is bitten, follow the beeping and the blue arrows and touch them before the 10 seconds run out.
  • Every cure gives 25 coins and 40 XP.
  • If another doctor cures you, you lose your healing power.
Zombie

Infect everyone

  • Touch survivors to bite them.
  • Hear footsteps and stones, and see sound waves from nearby survivors.
  • Zombies are a bit slower than survivors, so use your abilities and work together.
  • A survivor who turns keeps playing as a zombie.
Patient Zero

The first zombie

"It's you. Go infect everyone!" Patient Zero is picked at random at the start. Each match opens with a different short story: a test tube broke in Lab 3, a glowing rock fell in the parking lot, a can of beans from 1987 in the cafeteria, and more.

Controls

On a phone you can play in portrait or landscape. On a computer you play in the browser with the keyboard.

Phone

  • Joystick on the side of the screen: drag your thumb to move.
  • Ability buttons: up to 3 abilities you picked. A button that is charging shows how long is left.
  • Task button shows up when you stand next to a task station.
  • Stone button throws your stone (survivors only, once per match).
  • The phone vibrates when something big happens, like a bite or a cure.

Five tasks

Each task station (the blue tile on the map) opens a short mini-game. You can't move while doing a task, so check that no zombie is close first.

Connect Wires

Join each colored wire to the plug of the same color.

Fix the Machine

Hit the gears again and again until the machine runs.

Download Data

Start the download and stay at the station until it finishes.

Empty the Trash

Pull the lever until the bin is empty.

Check Temperature

Set the temperature to exactly the target number.

Tip

The Toolbox upgrade and the Engineer skin power make tasks faster. A task is worth 15 coins and makes the round shorter for every survivor.

Four game modes

Your progress (XP, coins and daily missions) counts in every mode.

Classic 10 minutes

The main mode. Survivors vs. zombies, up to 10 players per room, with bots filling empty spots. Everything in "How to play" above.

Tournaments Knockout

8 players, half zombies and half survivors. You move on only if the team you started on wins: 8, then 4, then a final of 2. Each round lasts 3 minutes, survivors are 10% faster and zombies 10% slower. Official maps only.

Play with friends

Sign up with an account to play online. With no internet, the game switches to offline mode and you play against bots.

Open a room

  1. Tap "Create room".
  2. Send your friends the room code (5 letters).
  3. Friends tap "Join with code" and type it in.
  4. The host starts the match. Bots fill empty spots, up to 10 players.

You can also join open rooms from the list.

Progress and shop

Every match gives XP and coins. Level up (up to level 50), and higher levels unlock new items in the shop.

XP and levels

For playing, winning, tasks, infections and cures.

Coins

You start with 300. Earn more in matches, daily missions and tournaments.

Medals

From tournaments and obstacle courses. They buy special items, and each medal can be swapped for 50 coins.

Daily and weekly missions

New challenges every day and every week, like "Play 3 matches", "Infect 3 survivors", "Finish 30 tasks" or "Run an obstacle course". Each one pays coins and XP.

Skins with powers

Some skins give a special power: Tank blocks the first infection, Ghost is harder to spot, Dr. Bandage heals faster, Ninja is quick, Alpha Zombie has a faster Dash, and more.

Skin Designer

Design your own character: colors, pattern and eyes. Skins you design are just for looks, with no special power, so the game stays fair.

Outfits

More than 50 head items: cowboy hat, crown, alien, T-rex, diamond and more. Some can only be bought with medals.

Upgrades

11 upgrades, each up to level 5: Running Shoes, Fast Charger, Strong Flashlight, Toolbox, Long Arms, Quiet Steps, First Aid Kit, and for zombies Rotten Legs, Dead Eyes, Keen Ears and Blood Pump.

Zombie filters

Color the screen while you are a zombie, and each filter gives a small bonus: Night Vision, Blood Moon (+5% speed), Thermal, Toxic, Frost, X-Ray, Predator and more.

The game is not in the app stores, so on phones you install it straight from the file. It takes a minute. On a computer there is nothing to install.

Common questions

Does the game cost money?

No. The game is free and has no real-money purchases. Everything in the shop is bought with coins and medals you earn by playing.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. With no connection the game switches to offline mode and you play against bots. Playing with friends, tournaments and sharing maps need internet.

How many people can play together?

Up to 10 in a normal room, and 8 in a tournament. If there aren't enough friends, bots fill the empty spots.

Can phone and computer players play together?

Yes. Everyone joins the same room with the room code, no matter which device they use.

I got bitten. Now what?

You are frozen for 10 seconds. If a doctor touches you in that time, you are human again. If not, you turn into a zombie and keep playing on the other team.

How do I know who the doctor is?

The role screen at the start tells you if you are a doctor ("You're a doctor!"). Doctors hear beeping and see blue arrows that lead to a bitten survivor.

Which languages does the game have?

16: English, Hebrew, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Italian, Turkish, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Polish and Dutch. Change it in Settings.
